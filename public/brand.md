# SkillDash identity

Original identity created for SkillDash by Ajnas N B. The interlocking S draws on library spines and forward motion. The website uses evergreen; launch media uses warm gold on graphite.

Prompt: Create an original compact geometric S from two interlocking file/library spines with a subtle forward-slant cut, followed by the exact wordmark “SkillDash.” Motorsport precision, warm gold #D6B56A or deep evergreen #166347, flat vector-like silhouette, clean transparent background, no robot, car, shield, tagline or glow.
